How a change goes live

There's no approval queue. What you publish is live within a minute, and every version is kept, so anything can be put back.

1You edit

Press a card or pencil above and fill in the form. The preview shows what visitors will see.

2You publish

Press Publish. The change is saved to the site's history with your name on it.

3It's live

Vercel rebuilds the site. The card here says Going live… and turns to Live within about a minute. Made a mistake? Edit again, or ask Rashdan to roll back.
